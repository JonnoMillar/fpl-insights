# Repetitive and unnecessary copy: audit and ranked cuts (21 October 2026)

**Summary**
- **64 issues** across the four tabs and the page-wide header/footer, of which 22 are marked *judgement call*. Source: `.cache/ui/after.html` (GW7 build) read against `dashboard.py`, `cards_*.py`, `components.py`, `ticker.py`, `transfers.py`, `analysis.py`, `scout.py` and the feature `.js` files. Nothing was edited.
- **Worst three:** (1) the "published squad / check your actual team" caveat is printed in seven places and is also *overwritten by JS* in the one place meant to hold it (P1, X1); (2) the transfer-plan rules (3-point margin, 4-point hit, 1.5 banked transfer, enabler rule, "later weeks less certain") are written out three times across the Planning tab (L1, X3); (3) the Scout "Worth a look" and "Fixture runs" chapters are each followed by a card with the same or near-same title, with the chapter lede, the card lede and the hidden "i" note all saying "each ranked on its own" (C1-C3).
- **Pattern:** the card-head pairs a visible lede (`.subvis`) with a hidden "i" note (`.sub`) that repeats it. At least ten cards do this (L4, L14, L15, M3, M4, M5, C2, C5; X8). Fix once by deleting the hidden twin whenever the visible lede already says it.
- **Visible vs hidden:** rows say where the text sits. *Visible* = on the face of the page by default. *Behind "i"* = `.sub`/`.note` with `hidden`, shown only after a click. Rank is by visual impact, so visible repeats come first.
- **Accuracy findings spotted on the way** (copy that is wrong, not just repeated): "Nine readings" (S6, there are ten cards), "so the order weighs both" (M5, the This week list ranks by points only), "the eight of you" hard-coded (M6).

Constraints honoured: the three meaning-critical caveats (last *published* squad, double gameweeks unsupported, unavailable data flagged) each keep exactly one visible home (see X1, X2). No new features, no redesign; every proposal is a cut, a shorter rewrite or a merge.
