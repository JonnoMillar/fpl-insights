RULES

Meaning never rides on colour. Signs, arrows, words and shape carry it: +8.7, ▲ rising, "you own 3".
Faces, crests and icons first. Show the opponent's crest, not the player's own club.
Click to reveal, don't scroll to read. Weeks and options sit behind one control each.
One headline number per card, big. Plans show moves and chips, not weekly xP.
Never
IBM Plex Mono or any dotted zero. Small grey help lines. Pill tags.
Lilac, coloured card edges, green-edged bands, ticket stubs, big decorative gradients.
Charts squeezed into small spaces. A red-to-green rating scale.

Swipe deck · other transfer options B'S DECK, REBUILT ON C'S TRANSFER CARD

Each card is the transfer card in purple: old player greyed, new player standing on the photo panel's edge, opponent crest on a small glass plate, then the name and one big gain. Cards fan out behind, ranked by gain. Drag, use the arrows, or pick from the ranked list (desktop) or the row of faces (phone). A gentle foil shimmer runs across the purple and speeds up on hover.

Only for browsing options. The hero move and the captain stay single cards.

PLAYER IMAGES

Do: stand on the card's bottom edge.
Do: rings and glows go behind the player.
Don't: end a cut-out in a hard line mid-card.
Don't: draw rings or floor glows across the body.

Cut-outs break out of a card only on big hero cards. Small list rows use round faces.

MOTION

Week change. New stage content rises 14px and fades in, 500ms.
Deck. Cards travel 550ms. A drag past 70px (60px on phone) flips to the next card. The top card tilts with the drag.
Hover. Tiles lift 3px. Glass tiles catch a light sweep, 900ms.
One easing everywhere: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Reduced motion turns all of it off.

SHAPE & DEPTH

Corners. 28 sections and the stage, 24 cards, 18–22 tiles and photo panels, 12–14 strips and plates.
Shadows. Soft and purple-tinted on paper. White cards barely lift; the selected week and the deck lift the most.
Glass. Only on night: crest tiles, the deck's opponent plate, the phone tab bar.
Texture. Faint pitch markings (7–8% opacity) on the stage, the green panel and purple headers. Nothing else.
