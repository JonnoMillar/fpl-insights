## Basis of the decision

Inspected the published site's Planning and Scout-related data, current source
code, handoff and research audit. The live dashboard at inspection was stamped
28 October 20:04 UTC and described **Savin Kluivert Rayan**, the other account.
Its highest-predicted XI explicitly isn't a budget-legal 15; its bank, used chips
and transfer instructions cannot be applied to this fresh account.

The isolated run loaded **669 players, 380 fixtures, 760 FFS club-gameweeks,
219 predicted starters and eight historical gameweeks**. All six source-health
lines passed. It considered **173 initially eligible candidates**. Four-, six-
and eight-week seeds, £99.5m/£100m budgets, captain-emphasised routes, balanced
no-£12m routes and Chelsea exclusions produced **25 distinct squads** after
single-swap refinement. No dependency, production model or dashboard build changed.

The comparison objective is discounted GW9–16 best-XI points, a second captain
share and small weighted-bench allowances. It is a **heuristic comparison**, not
a calibrated forecast of this account's season score or proof of global optimality.

not measured corrections. The selected squad had the lowest worst shortfall
within that limited comparison set (about 5.26 discounted points); it did not
dominate every scenario. The Chelsea-downside scenario favoured a Mbeumo route;
the persistent-role scenario improved Manzambi alternatives. Full local tables
are ignored in `.cache/sensitivity.json`; the captured inputs are also local.
